About us

Lumen is a graphic design team from BSCS-1C at Taguig City University. We sketch, test and refine every design together, from the first rough idea to the final file, because we believe good visual communication starts with listening.

Who we design for

Small food businesses, online sellers, student organizations and freelancers who need clear, memorable visuals without a big budget.

What we make: brand identity, social media graphics, posters, marketing materials, illustration and presentation layouts.

How we work

Collaboration
Every design is discussed, tested and improved by the whole team.

Design thinking
We ask what the message is and who it is for before we pick a color.

Attention to detail
Spacing, alignment and type are checked before anything is delivered.
